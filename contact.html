What to include

For a technical or catalogue issue, include the game title, the BrainrotGames URL, your device and browser, the date and time, and the steps that produced the issue. A screenshot can help if it does not reveal private information.

Games are delivered by third-party providers, so we may be able to correct a site listing or remove an invalid route while a provider controls the underlying game build. We review reports in that context and update site information when a correction is warranted.

Keep your information private

Do not send passwords, payment details, account recovery codes, full personal addresses or other sensitive information. Browser games on this site should not require that information for a technical report.

Feedback and public comments

BrainrotGames does not publish invented player comments, ratings or testimonials. If we later display community feedback, it will be clearly identified, based on an authentic submission and used with the contributor’s permission.
