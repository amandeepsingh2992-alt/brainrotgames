Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how BrainrotGames uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit or use our website.

This policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which explains more generally how information may be collected, used and shared through BrainrotGames.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites remember information, operate correctly, understand how visitors use the website and support certain advertising or other services.

Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, web beacons, device identifiers and other technologies that can store or access information on a browser or device.

2. How BrainrotGames Uses Cookies

BrainrotGames and third-party services operating on the website may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Supporting website functionality and security.
  • Understanding how visitors interact with the website.
  • Remembering certain technical or browser-related information.
  • Measuring website and game usage.
  • Supporting advertising and related technologies.

3. Third-Party Games and Services

Some games and related services available on BrainrotGames are provided through third-party platforms, including GamePix.

When a game is loaded or played, your browser may communicate with third-party game technology and distribution services. These services may use cookies, local storage, pixels, identifiers or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Because these services are operated by third parties, the technologies they use may change over time. BrainrotGames does not control the privacy or cookie practices of third-party services.

4. Advertising Cookies and Technologies

BrainrotGames may display advertisements supplied by Google and other third-party advertising providers.

Google and other advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons, advertising identifiers, IP addresses or similar technologies to serve, measure, personalize and improve advertising.

Google may use advertising cookies to help serve advertisements based on a user's visits to BrainrotGames and other websites.

Users can learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services by visiting How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services .

Users may also manage or opt out of certain personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings .

Other advertising providers or technology partners may have their own privacy and cookie policies and may provide their own mechanisms for managing advertising preferences.

5. Consent and Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be asked to provide consent before certain cookies or similar technologies are used for advertising or other purposes.

Where required, BrainrotGames may use a consent management mechanism to allow visitors to make choices regarding cookies, advertising personalization and related processing.

When Google advertising services are implemented on BrainrotGames, applicable Google consent requirements will be followed for users in relevant regions.

6. Managing Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow you to view, block or delete cookies through the browser's settings.

You can also manage certain advertising preferences through the advertising provider's available controls, including Google's Ads Settings .

Blocking or deleting certain cookies may affect the functionality or availability of some website features, games or advertising services.
