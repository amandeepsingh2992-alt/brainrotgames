Last updated: August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how BrainrotGames uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit or use our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites remember information, operate correctly, understand how visitors use the website and support certain advertising or other services.

2. How BrainrotGames Uses Cookies

BrainrotGames and third-party services operating on the website may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Supporting website functionality and security.
  • Understanding how visitors interact with the website.
  • Remembering certain technical or browser-related information.
  • Measuring website and game usage.
  • Supporting advertising and related technologies.

3. Third-Party Services

Some content and services available on BrainrotGames are provided by third parties, including game distribution and advertising partners.

These third parties may use cookies, pixels, local storage or similar technologies according to their own policies and applicable requirements.

Because these services are operated by third parties, the technologies they use may change over time.

4. Advertising Cookies

BrainrotGames may display advertisements supplied by third-party advertising partners. Advertising technologies may use cookies or similar identifiers to help deliver, measure and improve advertising.

Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be presented with choices regarding the processing of information for advertising purposes.

5. Embedded Games

Games on BrainrotGames may be delivered through third-party game platforms. When a game is loaded, the third-party platform may process technical information and use cookies or similar technologies in accordance with its own policies.

6. Managing Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow you to view, block or delete cookies through the browser's settings.

Blocking certain cookies may affect the functionality or availability of some website features, games or advertising services.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, third-party services, advertising technologies or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
