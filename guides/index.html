OUR EDITORIAL APPROACH

Games are the service; guides are our original layer

BrainrotGames uses third-party game distribution services for parts of its playable catalogue. Those games and their intellectual property belong to their respective rights holders. Our original contribution is the way we organize discovery, explain practical gameplay considerations and publish independent guidance for visitors.

We do not treat a feed title or provider description as a substitute for editorial information. Game availability can change, so our guides focus on durable advice rather than pretending that a third-party game feed is static.
