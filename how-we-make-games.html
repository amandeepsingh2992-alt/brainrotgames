OUR PROCESS

How BrainrotGames makes and maintains its games

BrainrotGames is moving from a third party catalogue toward small, first party browser games. The aim is simple: visitors should be able to see who made the playable experience, understand how it works, and report a problem to the people responsible for the surrounding site.

What is original here

Pulse Tap, Orbit Shift and Letter Ladder use game code, rules, interfaces and instructions created for BrainrotGames. They run directly in the page. They are not embedded from a game marketplace and do not require a user account to begin.

How a game is designed

  1. Start with one clear goal a visitor can understand before playing.
  2. Provide keyboard, mouse or touch controls where appropriate.
  3. Keep instructions beside the playable area.
  4. Test the page, route, controls and restart path before publishing.
  5. Use player reports to identify broken controls, unclear language or accessibility issues.

What we do not claim

We do not publish fake player ratings, fabricated comments, invented download numbers or claims that every device performs identically. A browser and device can affect controls and performance. Each game page explains the controls it actually supports.

Report a problem or idea

If a game control is unclear, a keyboard action fails or you have an idea for a better round, use the feedback and corrections page. Include the game name, your browser, device and what happened. Do not send passwords or personal account details.
