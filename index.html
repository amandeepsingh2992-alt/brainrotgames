CATALOGUE TRANSPARENCY

What BrainrotGames checks, and what it does not claim

Games, artwork and basic descriptions are supplied through a third-party distribution feed. Automated checks can catch broken routes and unavailable embeds. They do not replace playtesting every title or prove that every game works on every device.

FAQ

Before you play

Do I need to download a game?

Supported titles launch in the browser. Do not install an unrelated download just because a third-party prompt asks for one; check the game’s own instructions first.

Will every game work on a phone?

No. Some titles work best with a keyboard, mouse or larger screen. Test a short round and use the device guide if the layout or controls feel unsuitable.

Why is a game unavailable?

Providers can update or remove games. Refresh once, then try a different title. If several games fail, use the loading-problems guide to isolate the cause.
