ORIGINAL DODGER

Orbit Shift

Move the green ship left and right. Avoid the falling orbs for as long as you can. The round begins when you use a control.

Score: 0Ready

Controls

Use Left and Right arrow keys, A and D, or the on screen buttons. The game supports touch and keyboard. Refresh the page to start a new round after a collision.

Design note

Orbit Shift is made for short sessions. It has no account, no third party player and no collection of game progress outside your current browser tab.
