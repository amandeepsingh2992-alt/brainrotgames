ORIGINAL REACTION GAME

Pulse Tap

Wait for the panel to turn green, then tap or click it. Starting early resets the round. Your best result is stored only in this browser.

Press the panel to begin.

How to play

Press Start. Keep watching the panel. When it becomes green, press it once. The result is shown in milliseconds. A very early press does not count, so wait for the visual signal.

Accessibility and controls

The whole game uses one large button and works with mouse, touch, Enter and Space. If motion or colour makes the signal hard to follow, the status message also changes from “Wait” to “Tap now”.
